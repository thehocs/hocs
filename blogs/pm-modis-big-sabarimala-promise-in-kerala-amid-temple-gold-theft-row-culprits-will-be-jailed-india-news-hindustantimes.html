PM Modi's Promise to Sabarimala Temple Amid Gold Theft Row: 'Culprits Will Be Jailed'

2026-01-23 · 5 min read · HOCS team

PM Narendra Modi has made a significant announcement regarding the Sabarimala temple, emphasizing his commitment to restore religious freedom and tourism security in Kerala.

In a statement addressing concerns over gold theft at the Sabarimala temple, PM Narendra Modi has pledged that those caught stealing from the sacred shrine will face legal consequences. This move is seen as a measure to ensure safety and integrity for both pilgrims and tourists visiting the site during its annual period.

The Sabarimala Temple and its Significance

The Sabarimala temple, located in Pathanamthitta district of Kerala, is one of the most significant pilgrimage sites in India. It is dedicated to Lord Ayyappa, a deity who symbolizes divine love between men and women.

Background on Gold Theft at Sabarimala

In recent years, there have been incidents where gold ornaments were stolen from the temple premises during its annual pilgrimage season. These thefts have raised serious concerns about security measures and religious freedom in the temple complex.

PM Narendra Modi's Commitment to Sabarimala

Recognizing the importance of maintaining religious integrity and safety, PM Narendra Modi has made a strong promise. 'Culprits involved in any thefts at Sabarimala will be jailed,' he said.

Impact on Religious Freedom and Tourism Security

By ensuring that the perpetrators are apprehended and punished, PM Modi aims to restore confidence among devotees. This promise is not just about law enforcement but also emphasizes the importance of upholding religious values in public spaces.

Conclusion: Restoring Trust and Security

The commitment made by PM Narendra Modi comes at a crucial time, where maintaining religious harmony and ensuring safety are paramount. His assurance that justice will be served stands as a strong statement for all those who visit the Sabarimala temple.

Conclusion

PM Narendra Modi's promise to jail perpetrators caught in thefts at Sabarimala reaffirms his dedication to upholding religious freedom and security. This move not only addresses current issues but also sets an example of accountability for any future occurrences.
