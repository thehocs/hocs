Mohan Bhagwat: Erasing Caste Mindset is Key to Ending Discrimination

2026-01-18 · 4 min read · HOCS team

In a recent interview, RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat has emphasized the role of societal mindset in overcoming caste-based discrimination.

RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat has called for a shift towards eliminating caste-based discrimination from people's minds to foster a more inclusive society. He advocates that changing attitudes and beliefs is crucial for ending such forms of prejudice.

The Importance of Mindset Change

Mohan Bhagwat believes that the root cause of caste-based discrimination lies in societal mindsets and perceptions. It's not enough to legislate against discriminatory practices; people need to understand and internalize a more equitable worldview.

Promoting Caste Equality

The RSS chief stresses the importance of promoting equality across all social strata. By actively discouraging any form of discrimination or superiority complex based on caste, individuals can contribute to building a truly united nation.

Breaking Generational Mentality

Bhagwat also highlights the need for breaking down the generational mindset that perpetuates caste-based distinctions. By educating future generations about the futility of such divisions, we can move towards a more just and inclusive society.

Building Bridges Across Differences

He advocates for initiatives that bring together people from different castes to foster mutual respect and understanding. This includes encouraging inter-caste marriages and social events that highlight shared values over caste differences.

Education as a Tool

The RSS chief recommends strengthening educational systems with curriculum reforms that emphasize the importance of valuing diversity. Such programs can help create a new generation committed to societal inclusivity.

Conclusion

To truly end caste-based discrimination, Mohan Bhagwat argues, requires not just legal measures but also transformative change at the personal and societal levels. By encouraging a mindset shift towards equality, we pave the way for a more harmonious society.
