Names of 2.89 Crore Voters Missing in Uttar Pradesh Draft Electoral Roll After SIR: ECI

2026-01-08 · 7 min read · HOCS team

The recent draft electoral roll for Uttar Pradesh reveals a significant number of missing voter names post the Special Summary Revision (SSR). This development calls for urgent attention from voters and authorities alike.

The Election Commission of India (ECI) has announced that approximately 2.89 crore voters' names are missing from the recently published draft electoral roll of Uttar Pradesh following the Special Summary Revision (SSR). This revelation raises concerns about voter disenfranchisement in the state, highlighting the importance of timely voter verification and correction processes ahead of crucial elections.

Understanding the Special Summary Revision (SSR)

The Special Summary Revision is a periodic update of the electoral roll conducted by the Election Commission of India to ensure accuracy in voters' lists. It involves adding eligible voters, removing deceased or duplicate entries, and updating existing voter information. The SSR aims to maintain an updated and transparent voter database before major elections.

Scope of Missing Voter Names in Uttar Pradesh

After the latest SSR process in Uttar Pradesh, the ECI found that about 2.89 crore voter names are missing from the draft electoral roll. This figure represents a substantial portion of the state's electorate, impacting millions of citizens' ability to participate in upcoming elections if not rectified promptly.

Reasons for Voter Name Omission

Several factors may contribute to the omission of voter names, including errors during data compilation, outdated records, migration, and failure of voters to update their details. Administrative challenges during the SSR and COVID-19 disruptions may have also contributed to these discrepancies.

Implications for Uttar Pradesh Voters

Missing names in the electoral rolls risk disenfranchising eligible voters, potentially distorting democratic participation. It can also lead to confusion and mistrust in the electoral process. Voters whose names are missing will be unable to cast ballots unless corrective measures are undertaken swiftly.

How Voters Can Verify and Rectify Their Details

The Election Commission encourages all voters to check their names on the draft electoral roll using the official ECI website or the voter helpline app. If a voter’s name is missing, they should immediately apply for inclusion by submitting requisite documents at their local electoral office before the final roll publication deadline.

Conclusion

The omission of 2.89 crore voter names from Uttar Pradesh's draft electoral roll post-SSR is a significant concern. Immediate action from both the Election Commission and voters is essential to ensure all eligible citizens have the right to vote. Checking and updating voter details promptly will uphold the integrity of the democratic process in the state.
