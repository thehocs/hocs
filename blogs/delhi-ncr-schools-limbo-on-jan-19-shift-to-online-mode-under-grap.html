Delhi-NCR Schools Expected to Be in Limbo as Jan. 19 Closes, Online Mode Possible Under GRAP 4 - The Indian Express

2026-01-18 · 5 min read · HOCS team

Delhi-NCR schools are set to face closure on January 19, creating confusion among students and parents.

As of the latest updates from The Indian Express, Delhi-NCR schools are likely to be shut down on January 19. This move comes under GRAP 4 guidelines which suggest a shift towards online learning for some schools in response to unforeseen circumstances.

GRAP 4 and its Role in the Current Situation

The Government of India has issued Guidelines for Re-Opening Schools (GRAP) aimed at providing a safe environment for students. GRAP 4 specifically addresses scenarios where schools are likely to be shut down temporarily.

School Closure on January 19

Based on the latest information from The Indian Express, the Government of Delhi has announced that all schools in the National Capital Region (NCR) will remain closed on January 19. This decision was made to prevent any spread of infections and ensure the health and safety of students.

Online Mode as a Possible Solution

Under GRAP 4, there are suggestions that schools may be allowed to transition to online mode if circumstances warrant it. This could provide an immediate solution to keep students engaged and learning despite the closure.

Concerns Among Students and Parents

The announcement of school closures is causing concern among both students and parents. Many families are already preparing for potential disruptions in their daily routines, which could lead to financial strain due to schooling expenses.

Local Authorities' Perspective

Local authorities have been quick to react by providing support services such as food deliveries and virtual counseling sessions. They aim to ease the situation for students and parents affected by this sudden decision.

Conclusion

While school closures are an unfortunate reality in light of current circumstances, efforts are being made to mitigate their impact through alternative learning methods and supportive measures from local authorities.
