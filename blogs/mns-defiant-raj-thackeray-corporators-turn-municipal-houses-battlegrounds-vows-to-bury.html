Raj Thackeray's MNS: Corporators Must Turn Municipal Houses into Battlegrounds - Times of India

2026-01-19 · 5 min read · HOCS team

In a fresh show of defiance, MNS leader Raj Thackeray has directed corporators to make municipal houses their battleground, with warnings that resistance will be met with severe consequences.

In an exclusive interview, MNS chief Raj Thackeray expressed his anger towards corporators for not adhering to the party's demands regarding municipal houses. The party leader outlined a series of actions they plan to take if resistance continues.

Thackeray's Stance on Corporators and Municipal Houses

Raj Thackeray, in an interview with the Times of India, highlighted his stance on corporators. He stated that these entities have been violating agreements made by MNS regarding municipal houses.

MNS's Action Plan for Corporators

Thackeray mentioned several steps the party will take if the corporators continue to obstruct their plans with regards to municipal housing. These include issuing warnings, imposing penalties, and even physical actions like demolitions.

Raj Thackeray's Warning Against Resistance

The MNS chief reiterated his message that resistance will not be tolerated by the party. Corporators who disobey the instructions or obstruct MNS’s plans will face serious consequences, with no room for negotiation.

Municipal Houses and Their Importance in Society

Thackeray stressed the importance of municipal houses to the society's welfare. He maintained that these facilities are essential for providing shelter, education, and healthcare to the underprivileged sections of society.

Raj Thackeray's Commitment to Ensuring Social Equity

The MNS chief emphasized their commitment to social equity by ensuring that municipal houses reach all deserving segments of the population. He vowed not only to provide these facilities but also to maintain them in good condition.

Conclusion

In summary, Raj Thackeray's MNS has issued a clear warning to corporators who refuse to comply with their demands concerning municipal housing. The party is prepared to take strong actions to ensure that the underprivileged sections of society have access to these essential facilities.
