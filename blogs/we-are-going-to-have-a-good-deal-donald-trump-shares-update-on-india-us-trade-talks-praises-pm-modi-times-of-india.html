Donald Trump Shares Update on India-US Trade Talks: Praises PM Modi

2026-01-22 · 4 min read · HOCS team

President Trump shares an update on the India-US trade talks and praises Prime Minister Modi for his leadership in fostering a positive relationship.

The United States has expressed optimism about upcoming India-US trade discussions, with President Donald Trump praising Prime Minister Narendra Modi's efforts to improve diplomatic ties. This comes as part of ongoing negotiations aimed at resolving long-standing economic disparities between the two nations.

President Trump’s Update on Talks

In a recent tweet, President Donald Trump highlighted the progress made during India-US trade talks and indicated that both countries are confident about reaching a favorable agreement. The president emphasized that the discussions have been constructive and that there is significant room for improvement in areas such as agriculture and manufacturing.

Praising PM Modi

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has been commended by President Trump for his leadership during these talks. In a separate statement, Trump praised Modi's commitment to fostering economic growth and highlighted the importance of continued collaboration between India and the United States in various sectors.

Progress Made

Trade experts have noted that both sides have made substantial progress in resolving contentious issues. For instance, discussions on reducing tariffs on agricultural products have been fruitful, with both nations showing willingness to find mutually beneficial solutions. This progress is seen as a positive step towards building stronger economic ties.

Ongoing Challenges

Despite the progress made, there are still several challenges that need to be addressed before an agreement can be finalized. These include issues related to technology transfers and intellectual property rights in sectors such as semiconductors and pharmaceuticals. Both governments have expressed a desire to resolve these contentious areas swiftly.

Public Sentiment

The latest developments on India-US trade talks have been welcomed by public opinion in both countries. In India, Modi's efforts to improve economic relations with the United States are viewed positively as they contribute to overall growth and stability. Similarly, in the United States, Trump’s confidence in the ongoing talks is seen as encouraging for businesses operating across industries.

Conclusion

As the Indian-US trade relationship continues to evolve, both governments remain committed to finding mutually beneficial solutions that strengthen economic ties while also ensuring fair treatment of all stakeholders. With President Trump's recent update and Modi’s continued leadership, there is optimism that these negotiations will yield positive results for the future of bilateral relations.
