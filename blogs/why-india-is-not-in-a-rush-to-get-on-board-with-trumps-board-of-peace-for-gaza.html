Why India is not in a Rush to Join Trump's 'Board of Peace' for Gaza | India News - Hindustan Times

2026-01-20 · 5 min read · HOCS team

In a time when international conflicts are increasingly complex and require diplomatic efforts, the proposal of President Donald Trump for a Board of Peace to resolve the Israel-Palestine conflict is intriguing.

The concept of a Board of Peace to address the ongoing conflict between Israel and Palestine has gained attention from various global leaders. Amidst this context, India's stance on such initiatives remains guarded and cautious.

Understanding India’s Position in International Conflicts

India has a history of participating in international forums related to peace negotiations but is selective about its involvement. The country often advocates for diplomatic solutions that respect the sovereignty and territorial integrity of all parties involved.

The Role of Sovereignty and Security

One of India's primary concerns is ensuring that any resolution does not compromise its strategic interests or violate the principle of non-interference. This belief has guided India’s stance in numerous conflicts around the world, including Kashmir.

India’s Engagement with Peace Processes

In recent years, India has been actively involved in various peace processes led by international bodies such as UNESCO and UNICEF. These engagements have often focused on areas like education, health, and women's empowerment rather than direct conflict resolution.

The Israel-Palestine Conflict and Its Complexity

While India has shown interest in facilitating dialogue between parties, it is also aware of the intricate nature of the Israel-Palestine conflict. The situation involves geopolitical interests, religious sensitivities, and historical grievances.

India’s Stance on Trump's Initiative

The proposal by President Donald Trump for a Board of Peace has been met with caution. India understands the urgency of addressing this conflict but believes that any solution must be inclusive and respect the autonomy of all parties involved.

Conclusion

India’s cautious stance towards international peace initiatives, including those led by global leaders like President Donald Trump, underscores its commitment to maintaining diplomatic neutrality while advocating for sustainable peace solutions.
