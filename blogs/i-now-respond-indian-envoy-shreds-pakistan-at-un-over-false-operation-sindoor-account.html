I Now Respond: Indian Envoy Shreds Pakistan at UN Over 'False' Operation Sindoor Account | India News - Hindustan Times

2026-01-27 · 5 min read · HOCS team

In a sharp rebuttal, India’s UN envoy Syed Akbaruddin took to the United Nations podium to defend his nation and rebuke Pakistan for spreading false information about an alleged attack during an operation.

Syed Akbaruddin, India's Permanent Representative to the United Nations (UN), addressed the General Assembly session with a powerful statement. He emphatically refuted claims made by Pakistani officials regarding the 'Operation Sindoor' and accused them of perpetuating misinformation.

Opening Address

Syed Akbaruddin's opening remarks at the UN General Assembly session were direct and clear. He began with a stark reminder to his audience that Pakistan was not the first nation to raise concerns about any military operations conducted by India.

Charges Against Pakistan

He then proceeded to outline several points of contention, beginning with allegations concerning Operation Sindoor. Akbaruddin stated emphatically: 'I am here today to present a sharp rebuttal to false narratives that have been circulating in the international media about our military operations.'

Criticism of Pakistani Tactics

The envoy also criticized Pakistan for its handling and dissemination of information. He accused them of 'fabricating facts' and using misleading accounts as a means to divert attention from their own shortcomings.

India's Definitive Response

Akbaruddin underscored the importance of factual reporting and the right to respond to such allegations. He stated, 'It is our duty, as a responsible nation, to ensure that we provide accurate information on military operations conducted within our borders.'

Conclusion and Future Outlook

The Permanent Representative concluded his speech by emphasizing India's commitment to maintaining transparency in its dealings with the international community. He also outlined possible future steps, including increased collaboration with UN bodies for addressing similar issues.

Conclusion

In a strong stance against misinformation and disinformation campaigns by Pakistan, Syed Akbaruddin’s address at the UN General Assembly was a significant moment in international diplomacy. His authoritative rebuttal not only defended India's actions but also highlighted the need for enhanced trust between nations.
