PM Mark Carney's Response to Trump’s 100% Tariff Threat: 'We're Buying Canadian'

2026-01-26 · 5 min read · HOCS team

In a recent statement addressing Canada's economic relationship with the United States, Governor of the Bank of Canada Mark Carney delivered a strong defense against President Trump’s proposed punitive measures.

Governor of the Bank of Canada Mark Carney has issued a firm response to President Donald Trump’s threat of imposing a 100% tariff on goods imported from Canada. In this article, we provide insight into Carney's stance and Canada's strategy in response to US sanctions.

The Background: The NAFTA Deal

In March 2018, the United States and Canada reached a deal to replace the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) with a new agreement. This move was seen as an attempt by President Trump to address some of his concerns about trade imbalance between the US and Mexico.

President Trump's 100% Tariff Threat

Following Canada’s refusal to meet certain conditions outlined in the proposed replacement agreement, President Donald Trump announced plans for a 25% tariff on goods imported from Canada. He further threatened to escalate this issue to a full 100% tariff.

Carney's Defending Statement

In response to these threats, Governor of the Bank of Canada Mark Carney stated that 'We're Buying Canadian.' This statement aimed to reassure Canadians and international partners about Canada’s commitment to maintaining strong trade relations with its neighbor.

Canada's Economic Strategy

To counter Trump's tariffs, Canada has taken several steps. Firstly, they have increased their export of goods such as wood products and dairy products to the United States. Secondly, they are exploring new markets outside of the US, including in Europe and Asia.

Conclusion: A Complex Economic Landscape

The situation presents a complex economic landscape with potential implications for global trade and stability. As international leaders seek to negotiate and find solutions, both Canada and the United States must maintain diplomatic channels open to prevent any escalations.

Conclusion

In conclusion, Governor Mark Carney's response of 'We're Buying Canadian' highlights Canada's commitment to maintaining a strong economic relationship with its neighbor. While challenges remain, Canada is taking proactive steps towards diversifying their trade routes and seeking solutions through diplomacy.
