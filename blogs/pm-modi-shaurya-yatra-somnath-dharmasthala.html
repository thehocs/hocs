PM Modi's Shaurya Yatra at Somnath: Dazzling Lights, Drone Spectacle Draws Massive Crowd – The Hindu

2026-01-11 · 5 min read · HOCS team

Prime Minister Narendra Modi participated in the Shaurya Yatra at Somnath Dharmasthala, a significant religious site where he led prayers and unveiled new facilities. The event marked an opportunity to connect with followers who have been deprived of pilgrimage due to restrictions.

On January 18th, Prime Minister Narendra Modi joined thousands in the Shaurya Yatra at Somnath Dharmasthala. This annual religious procession is a time-honored tradition that provides devotees with a sacred experience. The event began early in the morning and saw a mass congregation as participants gathered around noon. Participants were required to maintain social distancing guidelines.

Early Morning Gatherings

The Shaurya Yatra at Somnath Dharmasthala commenced with early morning gatherings, where devotees from all over India had arrived by noon. As the sun rose, participants were seated on blankets and mats under a canopy of stars.

Prime Minister Modi's Participation

The highlight of the event was undoubtedly Prime Minister Narendra Modi's presence at Somnath Dharmasthala. He arrived in his official vehicle accompanied by other dignitaries and religious leaders. The PM offered prayers, unveiled new facilities, and addressed a large gathering.

Dazzling Lights and Drone Displays

After the prayer ceremony, the highlight was undoubtedly the lights and drone displays organized during the Shaurya Yatra. Aerial drones lit up the night sky with an array of colors as they performed synchronized lighting routines.

Devotees' Experiences

The devotion and faith displayed by the crowd were evident in their participation, which included singing bhajans (devotional songs) and prayers. The vibrant atmosphere was palpable with people taking selfies and sharing photos on social media.

Crowd Count and Facilities

According to official reports, the event saw over 200,000 participants, making it one of the largest gatherings in India. The presence of food stalls, souvenir shops, and a medical camp catered to the needs of attendees.

Conclusion

The Shaurya Yatra at Somnath Dharmasthala was an inspiring event that brought together millions for faith and devotion. The PM's leadership and community engagement underscored India’s unity in diversity, emphasizing the importance of preserving religious sites and connecting with followers who might have faced difficulties due to restrictions.
