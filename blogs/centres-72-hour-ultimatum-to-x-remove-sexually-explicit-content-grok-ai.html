Centre’s 72-Hour Ultimatum to X to Remove Sexually Explicit Content on Grok AI

2026-01-03 · 7 min read · HOCS team

The Indian government has taken a firm stance against sexually explicit content generated by Grok AI, demanding swift action from X to comply with content guidelines.

In a significant regulatory move, the Indian Centre has issued a 72-hour ultimatum to the social media platform X to remove sexually explicit content created by Grok AI. This directive highlights the growing challenges of moderating AI-generated content and the government's commitment to preventing misuse on digital platforms. As AI-generated imagery and content become more prevalent, governments worldwide are grappling with how to enforce ethical and legal standards online.

Background on Grok AI and X Platform

Grok AI is an advanced artificial intelligence tool integrated into the social media platform X, formerly known as Twitter. It enables users to generate images and text based on user prompts. While this innovation has attracted creative usage, it has also been exploited to produce and disseminate sexually explicit content, raising concerns about content moderation and platform responsibility.

Government's Concerns over Sexually Explicit Content

The Centre's primary concern revolves around the rise of sexually explicit material generated through Grok AI without adequate filters or controls. Such content is considered harmful, especially as it may be easily accessible by minors and can potentially violate India's laws on obscenity and decency on digital platforms.

The 72-Hour Ultimatum and Its Implications

The ultimatum mandates X to take down all sexually explicit content produced by Grok AI within 72 hours or face legal consequences. This strict deadline reflects the urgency with which the government is addressing digital content safety and the expectation that platforms proactively manage AI-generated content responsibly.

Challenges in Regulating AI-Generated Content

Regulating AI-generated content, especially on platforms like X, presents unique challenges. AI can autonomously create content in vast volumes, making manual oversight difficult. Implementing effective filters, ethical guidelines, and quick response mechanisms is essential to prevent misuse without stifling innovation.

Future of AI Content Moderation in India

This ultimatum marks a pivotal moment in India's approach to governing AI-generated digital content. Moving forward, it is anticipated that stronger policies, collaboration between tech companies and regulators, and improved AI ethics frameworks will emerge to ensure content safety. Platforms like X will likely need to invest in advanced moderation technologies to comply with legal standards.

Conclusion

The Centre’s 72-hour ultimatum to X serves as a critical reminder of the responsibilities platforms bear in controlling AI-generated content. As AI becomes more integrated into social media, effective regulation and ethical content management will be key to safeguarding users and maintaining digital integrity in India.
