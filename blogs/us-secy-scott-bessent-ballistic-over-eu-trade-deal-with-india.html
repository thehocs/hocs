US Secretary Scott Bessent's Ballistic Response to EU Trade Deal with India: A Financial War Against Themselves Through Russian Oil

2026-01-26 · 5 min read · HOCS team

US Secretary Scott Bessent has issued a strong warning regarding the proposed trade deal between the European Union and India.

US Secretary Scott Bessent of the State Department has criticized the EU's proposed economic partnership agreement with India. His stance reflects deep concerns about potential geopolitical implications, particularly relating to energy security and sanctions on Russia. This article delves into the specifics of his critique and its broader context.

Background of the Proposed EU-India Trade Deal

The proposed trade deal between the European Union and India aims at enhancing economic cooperation, increasing mutual market access for goods and services, and deepening strategic ties. However, Bessent’s critique points out that this deal could inadvertently exacerbate existing challenges.

Bessent's Disapproval of Russian Oil

In his press conference, Secretary Bessent highlighted the EU-India trade agreement as a means for Russia to circumvent sanctions against it. He cited reliance on Russian energy resources such as oil and gas, which could undermine Western efforts aimed at isolating Russia economically.

Potential Financial Implications

Bessent further emphasized the potential economic impact of integrating with an energy regime dominated by Russia. This is seen as a threat to the collective European financial interests and security in the face of ongoing sanctions against Russia, which include trade embargoes and asset freezes.

Energy Security Concerns

The Secretary’s remarks also touch on energy security issues. India's reliance on Russian oil for around 12% of its total imports indicates how any deal with the EU could be seen as a risky financial decision, given the geopolitical risks associated with Russia's energy exports.

Bessent’s Warning to European Partners

Secretary Bessant urged caution in the proposed deal, warning that such agreements could inadvertently support Russia and potentially jeopardize Western sanctions on it. He stressed that economic cooperation must not come at the expense of geopolitical integrity.

Conclusion

In sum, Secretary Scott Bessent’s criticism underscores the complex interplay between economic partnerships, energy security, and geopolitical stability in international relations. The EU-India trade deal now faces significant scrutiny with his remarks serving as a clear warning to all parties involved.
