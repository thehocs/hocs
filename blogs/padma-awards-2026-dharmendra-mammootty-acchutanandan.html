Padma Awards 2026: Dharmendra and Achuthanandan Awarded Posthumously, Mammootty Recognized with Padma Bhushan - The Hindu

2026-01-25 · 3 min read · HOCS team

The Padma Awards ceremony for the year 2026 has been live-streamed on The Hindu, marking significant recognitions in both Indian politics and entertainment.

On January 25, 2026, the prestigious Padma Awards ceremony was held as part of The Hindu's live streaming event. Among the notable awardees were Dharmendra, who was awarded the prestigious Padma Vibhushan posthumously for his contributions to Indian cinema and politics; former Kerala Chief Minister Achuthanandan, also honored with the same award in recognition of his service to the state; and actor Mammootty, who received the Padma Bhushan for his outstanding contributions to the arts.

Dharmendra: A Legacy in Indian Cinema

Dharmendra, a prominent figure in both Bollywood and Telugu cinema, is posthumously awarded the Padma Vibhushan for his exceptional contributions to the film industry. His career spanned decades, during which he starred in numerous critically acclaimed films that have left an indelible mark on Indian cinema.

Achuthanandan: A Leader with a Mission

Former Kerala Chief Minister Achuthanandan is recognized for his service to the state of Kerala. He has been instrumental in spearheading several developmental projects that have transformed the socio-economic landscape of the state. His award underscores the value placed on public service and community leadership.

Mammootty: A Cultural Treasure

Actor Mammootty, known for his versatility and depth as an actor, is honored with the Padma Bhushan. This accolade reflects his dedication to acting over several decades, during which he has not only entertained but also deeply influenced countless lives through his performances.

The Significance of Posthumous Awards

It's worth noting that Dharmendra and Achuthanandan were awarded posthumously, a decision often reserved for individuals who have made substantial contributions to society but are no longer alive. This gesture underscores the importance placed on their legacies.

The Role of The Hindu in Recognizing Excellence

The live-streaming of the Padma Awards ceremony by The Hindu is not only a testament to the publication's commitment to excellence but also serves as an opportunity for Indian society to recognize and celebrate these outstanding contributions.

Conclusion

The Padma Awards ceremony, marked in the digital space by The Hindu's live-streaming, honors Dharmendra, Achuthanandan, and Mammootty for their significant contributions. These recognitions not only celebrate individual achievements but also reinforce the importance of public service, community leadership, and artistic excellence.
