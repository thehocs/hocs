Exclusive: India to Slash Car Tariffs to 40% in Trade Deal with EU, Sources Say - Reuters

2026-01-26 · 6 min read · HOCS team

Sources within India's trade department have provided insights into the potential changes to tariffs.

Reuters is reporting that India and Europe may enter into a significant trade deal where car tariff reductions could be a key component. The details are still under negotiation but sources suggest a reduction to around 40%.

Global Trade Dynamics

In recent years, there has been a growing interest in global trade agreements as nations seek to integrate their economies and reduce dependency on foreign markets. This particular deal with the EU could serve as an important benchmark.

Current Tariff Landscape

Currently, India's car tariffs are significantly higher than those of European countries. This imbalance has been a source of contention between the two blocs, leading to ongoing negotiations aimed at achieving a more equitable market access scenario.

Potential Impact on Industry

If implemented, this tariff reduction could have substantial implications for both sides. For India, it might facilitate increased exports and attract foreign investments, while for the EU, it could lead to lower prices for consumers of Indian-manufactured vehicles.

Negotiations in Progress

The exact terms of this trade deal remain uncertain as the negotiations are still ongoing. However, what is certain is that if implemented, these tariff reductions would represent a major shift from previous policies and could signal broader changes to global economic relationships.

Uncertainty Surrounding Final Outcome

While there is optimism surrounding this potential deal, the final outcome remains uncertain. Various factors such as political stability, market demand dynamics, and long-standing trade disputes will play crucial roles in determining whether these tariffs are indeed slashed to 40%.

Conclusion

As negotiations continue between India and the EU, it is clear that this potential deal could reshape global economic relations. If successful, it represents a significant move towards greater market integration and reduced trade barriers.
