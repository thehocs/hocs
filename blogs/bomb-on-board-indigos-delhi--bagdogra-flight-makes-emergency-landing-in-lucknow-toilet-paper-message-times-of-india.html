IndiGo Flight Incident: Bomb Alert, Emergency Landing in Lucknow Amid Toilet Paper Message - Times of India Analysis

2026-01-18 · 4 min read · HOCS team

IndiGo flight BA588 encountered an unexpected bomb alert during its journey from Delhi to Bagdogra, resulting in a hurried emergency landing at the airport in Lucknow.

An IndiGo flight, Flight BA588, faced a severe incident on October 7th. The aircraft operated by the airline was en route from Delhi to Bagdogra when it received an alarming bomb alert message via a toilet paper sheet. This article examines the details of this extraordinary occurrence and its implications.

Initial Situation

IndiGo Flight BA588 took off from Delhi Airport at approximately 06:40 AM IST (Indian Standard Time). The aircraft was scheduled to land in Bagdogra, located about 720 km away. It carried over 100 passengers and crew members on board.

Unexpected Bomb Alert

Unbeknownst to the flight's operators, a threat had been anonymously conveyed through the toilet paper messages used by some of the passengers. The message was passed from passenger to passenger before reaching the aircraft cabin steward.

Immediate Action Taken

Upon receiving this sensitive information, the pilot immediately initiated emergency procedures and descended the plane's altitude. He communicated with air traffic control for further guidance while ensuring safety protocols were followed on board.

Emergency Landing in Lucknow

The flight was advised to head towards Lucknow Airport, which is closer to its destination Bagdogra and also has a shorter distance from the current position. The pilots decided to land there due to the threat's proximity. The aircraft touched down safely at 07:15 AM IST.

Impact on Passengers and Crew

All passengers were allowed to disembark under close supervision. The security personnel conducted a thorough search of the plane, which did not reveal any bomb. However, all passengers and crew members remained in safekeeping until further instructions from authorities.

Conclusion

IndiGo's swift response during this incident exemplified their commitment to passenger safety. Despite the distressing situation, they managed to ensure that everyone onboard reached a safe destination with minimal inconvenience.
