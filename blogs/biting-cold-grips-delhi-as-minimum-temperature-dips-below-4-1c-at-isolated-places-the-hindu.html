Biting Cold Hits Delhi with Minimum Temperature Falling Below 4.1°C in Isolated Areas - The Hindu

2026-01-12 · 5 min read · HOCS team

The harsh winter in Delhi has been severe, with temperatures dropping significantly and affecting daily life.

Delhi is experiencing its coldest spell of the season, with the minimum temperature reaching alarming lows. This article explores the impact of these frigid conditions on the city's residents and infrastructure.

The Severity of the Current Weather Conditions

Delhi has seen its lowest recorded temperatures in recent years, with some areas recording a minimum temperature below freezing. This situation poses significant challenges for citizens as well as the city's utilities.

Impact on Daily Routines

People are facing difficulties managing their daily routines due to extreme cold. Schools and offices have had to close temporarily, and public transport systems are operating with reduced schedules. This has led to increased congestion in the city's streets.

Infrastructure Challenges

The city’s water supply networks are under stress as ice formation can disrupt normal operations. Additionally, there is a risk of pipes bursting if temperatures remain below freezing for extended periods, causing disruptions and potential repairs.

Health Concerns

Residents have been advised to take extra precautions due to cold-related health issues such as hypothermia. The elderly and people with pre-existing medical conditions are particularly vulnerable during these colder weather periods.

Emergency Services Operations

Fire and emergency services in Delhi have reported a surge in demand for services related to frostbite and minor injuries caused by slipping on icy surfaces. They are working diligently to ensure the safety of their communities.

Conclusion

Delhi's current weather conditions highlight the need for proactive measures to mitigate the effects of severe cold. Authorities must work closely with residents, utilities, and healthcare providers to address these challenges effectively.
