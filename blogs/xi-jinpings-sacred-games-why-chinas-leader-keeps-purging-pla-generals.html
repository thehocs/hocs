Xi Jinping's 'Sacred Games': Why China's Leader Keeps Purging PLA Generals

2026-01-26 · 4 min read · HOCS team

China's leader Xi Jinping has been making headlines with his purge of high-ranking military officials. This article explores the reasons behind these purges and their impact on China’s defense landscape.

President Xi Jinping of China is not just a political leader but also an architect of modernizing the Chinese People's Liberation Army (PLA). His recent wave of purging generals has stirred both curiosity and concern among observers. This article delves into why these actions have been taking place and how they reflect broader strategic shifts in China’s military structure.

The Scale of the Purge

In recent months, Xi Jinping has ordered the dismissal or removal from office of over a dozen generals. This number is staggering and marks one of the most significant changes in China's military leadership since the country’s founding.

Who Is Affected?

The purges have affected multiple levels of PLA command, touching on everything from air force commanders to missile experts. The impact is felt not only within China but also at Chinese military bases around the world.

Motivations Behind the Purge

While there are various theories, one prominent factor driving these purges seems to be Xi Jinping’s desire for complete control over the PLA. This mirrors broader concerns about corruption and a need for reform within China's armed forces.

Modernization Efforts

As part of this purge, Xi Jinping is also working on modernizing the PLA in various ways. These include technological advancements, restructuring command structures, and increasing transparency across all military branches.

Implications for Global Security

Given the global reach of the Chinese military, these changes could have significant geopolitical implications. However, how these purges will affect China's foreign policy and its role in international security remains to be seen.

Conclusion

The purge of high-ranking PLA officials by President Xi Jinping underscores his commitment to reshaping China’s armed forces for modern times. As the world watches closely, it is crucial to understand not only the immediate effects but also the long-term implications these changes might have on Chinese security and global relations.
