No 'AI Toys', Think Big: Inside PM Modi's Closed-Door Meeting with Startups Ahead of IndiaAI Impact Summit

2026-01-09 · 7 min read · HOCS team

PM Modi's recent closed-door interaction with Indian startups set a bold tone for AI's role in India's growth story, urging innovators to aim beyond trivial AI applications.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi convened a closed-door meeting with leading Indian startups just before the IndiaAI Impact Summit, underscoring the need for substantial AI deployments that tackle real challenges. Moving away from gimmicky AI demos or 'AI toys,' the Prime Minister encouraged entrepreneurs to leverage AI's transformative potential for sectors like agriculture, healthcare, and education. This directive signals a strategic push towards positioning India as a global AI powerhouse.

PM Modi's Vision: Think Beyond 'AI Toys'

In the meeting, PM Modi stressed that AI innovation should not be confined to superficial or entertainment-based applications often dubbed 'AI toys.' Instead, he emphasized the importance of creating impactful AI solutions that solve pressing societal and economic problems. According to Modi, startups must channel their creativity to build scalable technologies that can drive India’s inclusive development agenda.

Startups Called to Address Key Sectors

The Prime Minister urged startups to focus AI efforts on sectors critical to India's growth such as agriculture, healthcare, fintech, education, and governance. By applying AI to optimize farming practices, enhance diagnostics, innovate financial services, and improve learning outcomes, startups can contribute to real change on a national scale. Modi's message was clear: AI should be a tool for broad societal progress, not just commercial novelty.

IndiaAI Impact Summit: A Platform to Showcase Serious AI

Scheduled shortly after this meeting, the IndiaAI Impact Summit was described as a significant platform for AI innovators to showcase serious work and access opportunities to partner with government and industry. The summit aims to foster collaboration between startups, policymakers, and investors to accelerate AI adoption in transformative ways, positioning India at the forefront of the global AI revolution.

Government Support for Scalable AI Innovations

PM Modi’s meeting also highlighted the government’s commitment to supporting startups through favorable policies, funding, and access to data infrastructure. This support aims to overcome typical hurdles such as resource constraints and regulatory challenges, enabling innovators to scale impactful AI solutions effectively across diverse regions and populations.

The Road Ahead: Innovation with Responsibility

Alongside urging boldness, the Prime Minister stressed responsible AI development that respects ethics, privacy, and transparency. This balanced approach ensures technology benefits society holistically while mitigating risks associated with unchecked AI deployment. For India to lead globally, startups must align innovation with values that safeguard citizen interests.

Conclusion

PM Modi's closed-door meeting with startups sent a powerful message: India's AI trajectory depends on meaningful, scalable innovations rather than gimmicks. By focusing on substantial AI applications that address core challenges, Indian startups can drive transformative change and establish the country as a global leader in AI technology. The IndiaAI Impact Summit will be the proving ground for this ambitious vision.
