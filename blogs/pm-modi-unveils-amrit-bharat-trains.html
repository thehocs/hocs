PM Modi Unveils Development Projects and Amrit Bharat Trains: Key Highlights from The Hindu

2026-01-18 · 4 min read · HOCS team

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has made significant announcements in Bengal, including the unveiling of several development projects and the launch of Amrit Bharat trains.

The Prime Minister Narendra Modi recently visited Bengal to unveil a wide range of development projects and the first three Amrit Bharat trains. Here are the key details and impact of these announcements.

Development Projects Worth ₹830 Crore Unveiled

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has unveiled a total of ₹830 crore in development projects in Bengal during his recent visit. These projects cover various sectors including healthcare, education, and infrastructure.

Launch of Three Amrit Bharat Trains

In addition to the development projects, Mr. Modi also launched three new Amrit Bharat trains. The Amrit Bharat trains are part of India's ambitious efforts towards improving public transportation and reducing traffic congestion in major cities.

Focus on Public Transportation

The launch of these trains aligns with Mr. Modi’s vision to improve the quality of life for citizens by enhancing public transport systems. These trains are expected to play a crucial role in facilitating connectivity and reducing travel times within Bengal.

Enhanced Healthcare Facilities

One of the development projects unveiled is an expanded healthcare facility which will provide advanced medical services, including specialized treatments for rare diseases. This project aims to make essential healthcare more accessible to people in rural and remote areas.

Sustainable Infrastructure Initiatives

Another notable initiative involves the construction of new sustainable infrastructure projects such as solar-powered charging stations at key locations. These measures are intended to promote environmental sustainability while improving connectivity in Bengal's urban centers.

Conclusion

PM Modi’s announcements in Bengal signal a commitment towards enhancing multiple sectors including transportation, healthcare, and infrastructure. The launch of these development projects and Amrit Bharat trains marks significant progress toward the government’s broader goals for socio-economic development.
