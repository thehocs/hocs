PM Modi Slams Congress for Ignoring Assam: 'Exploiting' State’s Chances?

2026-01-18 · 5 min read · HOCS team

Prime Minister Narendra Modi's remarks come at a time when Assam is facing several developmental challenges.

In his recent statements, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has unequivocally criticized the Indian National Congress (INC) for its handling of Assam. He accuses the party of neglecting crucial progress in the state and capitalizing on local issues to gain political traction.

Background: The State's Challenges

Assam has faced significant developmental challenges, including unemployment, poverty, and lack of access to basic amenities. These factors have led to unrest in various regions.

Modi’s Accusations Against Congress

In his remarks, Prime Minister Modi pointed out that the INC has not adequately addressed these challenges. Instead, they have exploited Assam's potential by focusing on local issues and pitting different communities against each other.

Developmental Initiatives

Despite the INC's criticism, there are developmental initiatives underway in Assam aimed at improving healthcare facilities, education, and infrastructure. However, Modi believes these efforts have not been sufficiently supported or promoted by the Congress leadership.

Critique on Election Tactics

Modi further highlighted that the INC has used various election tactics to gain support in Assam. He accused them of leveraging local issues and pitting communities against each other, rather than focusing on sustainable development and addressing long-standing challenges.

Impact on State Progress

The Prime Minister's comments underscore the need for a more inclusive approach to governance in Assam. He believes that effective leadership should focus on bringing different communities together and fostering long-term progress rather than exploiting existing tensions for political gains.

Conclusion

Prime Minister Narendra Modi's stance against Congress in Assam highlights the ongoing challenges faced by the state and the need for a more collaborative approach to governance. As India continues its journey towards unity, there is an increasing call for inclusive policies that benefit all communities.
